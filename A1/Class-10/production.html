Asking for & Giving Prices

Step into a vibrant international flea market! In 10 minutes, you and your partner will practice buying and selling items. Practice singular vs. plural demonstratives (this/that vs these/those), numbers, currencies, discounts, payment methods, and calculating change.

Mode & Role Setup

Select pair mode for classroom interaction or solo mode with speech synthesis.

10-Minute Activity Flow

Follow the time-chunked sequence for pair role-playing.

Review the Market Catalog. Check item price tags, singular/plural grammar rules, and currencies.

2. Round 1 Shopping

Student A asks Cards 1–10. Student B checks stall price sheet and responds with full A1 models.

Min 05:00 - 08:00
